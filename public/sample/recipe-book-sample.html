1. Lemon herb pasta

  • 200g spaghetti, zest of 1 lemon, 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, parmesan, fresh basil
Boil pasta until al dente. Sizzle garlic in oil, toss with lemon zest, pasta water and parmesan.

2. Apple oat crumble

  • 4 apples, 80g oats, 50g butter, 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp cinnamon, pinch of salt
Slice apples into a dish, rub oats with butter and honey, bake 25 min at 180°C until golden.
